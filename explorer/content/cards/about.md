The tours are compiled from the working tree, not hand-drawn. They read the Helm renders, Compose services, Kubernetes resources, scripts, config usage, workflows, and Renovate rules. Every card links back to the line that produced it.

The scope is intentionally two machines: **vps0** (public edge) and **srv0** (home cluster). `bigpc` appears only as an optional GPU worker inside srv0; `rpi` only as an optional camera source inside Frigate; `pc` is mentioned in passing as another Compose target.

**Pick a tour**, read the chapter panel, and click a card to inspect it. **Code explained** opens the implementing files. Use Continue or the chapter rail to move through the tour.
